# 1. Executive Summary

MZ Hospital Pvt Ltd is a multi-specialty healthcare provider offering outpatient, inpatient, diagnostic, pharmacy, telemedicine, and healthcare administration services.

As part of its operations, the hospital processes significant volumes of:

- Patient Personal Data
- Sensitive Health Information
- Employee Information
- Insurance Information
- Financial Information

To comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023, MZ Hospital has engaged a Privacy and Cybersecurity Consulting Team to conduct a comprehensive Data Privacy Assessment Program.

The assessment will evaluate how personal data is collected, processed, shared, retained, secured, and disposed of throughout the organization.

---

# 2. Engagement Objectives

The primary objectives are:

## Objective 1: Data Visibility

Identify all personal data processed by the organization.

## Objective 2: Data Flow Understanding

Understand how personal data moves across applications, departments, and third parties.

## Objective 3: Regulatory Compliance

Assess compliance against DPDPA requirements.

## Objective 4: Risk Identification

Identify privacy, security, and compliance risks.

## Objective 5: Remediation Planning

Develop a structured roadmap for compliance improvements.

# 7. Assessment Phases

## Phase 1 – Data Discovery

### Objective

Identify all personal data assets across the hospital.

### Activities

- Stakeholder Interviews
- Department Workshops
- System Review
- Data Inventory Collection
- Data Owner Identification
- Storage Location Identification
- Third-Party Data Sharing Identification

### Deliverables

- Data Discovery Register
- Data Inventory Register
- Data Asset Catalogue

---

## Phase 2 – Data Mapping

### Objective

Understand how personal data flows through the organization.

### Activities

- Application Mapping
- Data Flow Mapping
- Third-Party Flow Identification
- Internal Data Transfers
- External Data Transfers
- Cross-Border Transfer Analysis

### Deliverables

- Data Flow Diagrams
- Data Mapping Register
- System-to-System Flow Matrix

---

## Phase 3 – Record of Processing Activities (RoPA)

### Objective

Document all processing activities involving personal data.

### Activities

- Processing Activity Analysis
- Legal Basis Review
- Purpose Mapping
- Retention Review
- Data Principal Category Identification

### Deliverables

- RoPA Register
- Processing Activity Catalogue

---

## Phase 4 – DPIA

### Objective

Evaluate privacy risks associated with personal data processing.

### Activities

- Risk Identification
- Impact Analysis
- Likelihood Evaluation
- Existing Control Evaluation
- Residual Risk Assessment

### Deliverables

- DPIA Report
- Privacy Risk Register

---

## Phase 5 – DPDPA Gap Assessment

### Objective

Assess current practices against DPDPA requirements.

### Activities

- Consent Review
- Notice Review
- Data Subject Rights Review
- Data Retention Review
- Vendor Compliance Review
- Security Control Review

### Deliverables

- Gap Assessment Report
- Compliance Scorecard

---

## Phase 6 – Final Compliance Report

### Objective

Provide executive-level compliance assessment.

### Deliverables

- Executive Summary
- Compliance Status
- Risk Summary
- Key Findings
- Recommendations

---

## Phase 7 – Remediation Planning

### Objective

Develop compliance improvement roadmap.

### Deliverables

- Remediation Plan
- CAPA Register
- Implementation Roadmap
- Priority Matrix
