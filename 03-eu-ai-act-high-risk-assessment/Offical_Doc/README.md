# 🛡️ GDPR Compliance Toolkit

## 📖 Overview
This repository provides a **practical compliance framework** for SMEs and auditors to align with the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**.  
It includes:
- ✅ Condensed audit checklist
- 🔗 Cross‑references to official GDPR articles
- 📄 Links to the full regulation text (EUR‑Lex)

---

## ✅ GDPR Compliance Checklist

### 1. Data Mapping & Records
- [ ] Maintain records of all processing activities  
- Reference: **Article 30**

### 2. Lawful Basis
- [ ] Confirm lawful basis for each activity  
- Reference: **Article 6**

### 3. Privacy Notices
- [ ] Provide clear, accessible privacy notices  
- Reference: **Articles 12–14**

### 4. Consent Management
- [ ] Obtain explicit, informed consent  
- Reference: **Articles 7–8**

### 5. Data Subject Rights
- [ ] Implement processes for rights requests  
- Reference: **Articles 13–22**

### 6. Special Categories of Data
- [ ] Apply safeguards for sensitive data  
- Reference: **Article 9**

### 7. Security Measures
- [ ] Apply encryption, pseudonymisation, access controls  
- Reference: **Article 32**

### 8. Data Protection by Design/Default
- [ ] Integrate privacy into systems and processes  
- Reference: **Article 25**

### 9. DPIAs & Risk Assessments
- [ ] Conduct DPIAs for high‑risk processing  
- Reference: **Article 35**

### 10. Processor Contracts
- [ ] Ensure GDPR clauses in contracts  
- Reference: **Article 28**

### 11. DPO Appointment
- [ ] Appoint a Data Protection Officer if required  
- Reference: **Article 37**

### 12. Breach Response
- [ ] Establish incident response plan  
- [ ] Notify authority within 72 hours  
- Reference: **Articles 33–34**

### 13. International Transfers
- [ ] Use adequacy decisions, SCCs, or BCRs  
- Reference: **Chapter V (Articles 44–50)**

### 14. Training & Accountability
- [ ] Train staff on GDPR responsibilities  
- [ ] Maintain evidence of compliance activities  
- Reference: **Article 39**
