📝 DPDPA 2023 Discovery Questionnaire (Client Template)

**Section 1: Organization Profile **

Q1: What is your organization’s legal name and domain? (Short answer)

Q2: Have you identified yourself as a Data Fiduciary, Data Processor, or both? (Multiple choice: Fiduciary / Processor / Both)  
Evidence: Data-flow/role document

Section 2: Data Inventory & Processing
Q3: Have you prepared a complete inventory of digital personal data? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: Data inventory

Q4: Where is personal data collected in your organization? (Checkboxes: Website, Mobile App, CRM, HRMS, Hospital Information System, Others)  
Evidence: Application/process list

Q5: Why is each category of personal data processed? (Paragraph text)  
Evidence: Processing register

Q6: Is there a documented lawful purpose for each processing activity? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: Purpose register

Section 3: Privacy Notice & Consent
Q7: Do you provide a privacy notice to individuals? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: Privacy notice

Q8: Does the notice clearly explain data collected and purpose? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: Website/app forms

Q9: Is consent obtained where required? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: Consent records

Q10: Can consent be withdrawn easily? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: Consent-management process

Section 4: Data Principal Rights
Q11: Do you provide a mechanism for Data Principals to exercise their rights? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: Request mechanism

Q12: Is there a process for correction/update requests? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: SOP

Q13: Is there a grievance redressal mechanism? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: Grievance register

Section 5: Security Safeguards
Q14: Is personal data encrypted in transit? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: Configuration/evidence

Q15: Is personal data encrypted at rest? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: Cloud/database configuration

Q16: Are access controls based on least privilege? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: IAM report

Q17: Is MFA implemented for privileged access? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: IAM/MFA evidence

Q18: Are breach incidents recorded and investigated? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: Incident register

Section 6: Third-Party & Cross-Border Processing
Q19: Are third-party Data Processors identified? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: Vendor register

Q20: Are contracts with Data Processors reviewed for DPDP obligations? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: Contracts/DPAs

Q21: Are international data transfers identified and controlled? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: Transfer register

Section 7: Governance & Accountability
Q22: Is there senior-management accountability for DPDP compliance? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: Governance structure

Q23: Is there a documented DPDP compliance roadmap with owners and timelines? (Yes/No)  
Evidence: Compliance plan
