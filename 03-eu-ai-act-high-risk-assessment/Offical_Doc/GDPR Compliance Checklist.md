# ✅ GDPR Compliance Checklist

## 1. Data Mapping & Records
- [ ] Maintain records of all processing activities (Art. 30)
- [ ] Identify controllers, processors, and data flows

## 2. Lawful Basis
- [ ] Confirm lawful basis for each processing activity (Art. 6)
- [ ] Document justification (consent, contract, legal obligation, etc.)

## 3. Privacy Notices
- [ ] Provide clear, accessible privacy notices (Arts. 12–14)
- [ ] Update notices when processing changes

## 4. Consent Management
- [ ] Obtain explicit, informed consent (Arts. 7–8)
- [ ] Enable easy withdrawal of consent

## 5. Data Subject Rights
- [ ] Implement processes for access, rectification, erasure (Arts. 13–22)
- [ ] Support portability and objection requests

## 6. Special Categories of Data
- [ ] Apply safeguards for sensitive data (Art. 9)
- [ ] Limit processing unless exceptions apply

## 7. Security Measures
- [ ] Apply encryption, pseudonymisation, and access controls (Art. 32)
- [ ] Test resilience and recovery procedures

## 8. Data Protection by Design/Default
- [ ] Integrate privacy into systems and processes (Art. 25)
- [ ] Minimise data collection and retention

## 9. DPIAs & Risk Assessments
- [ ] Conduct Data Protection Impact Assessments for high-risk processing (Art. 35)
- [ ] Document mitigation measures

## 10. Processor Contracts
- [ ] Ensure contracts with processors include GDPR clauses (Art. 28)
- [ ] Audit vendor compliance regularly

## 11. DPO Appointment
- [ ] Appoint a Data Protection Officer if required (Art. 37)
- [ ] Define responsibilities and reporting lines

## 12. Breach Response
- [ ] Establish incident response plan (Arts. 33–34)
- [ ] Notify authority within 72 hours
- [ ] Inform affected individuals when required

## 13. International Transfers
- [ ] Use adequacy decisions, SCCs, or BCRs (Chapter V)
- [ ] Assess third-country risks

## 14. Training & Accountability
- [ ] Train staff on GDPR responsibilities (Art. 39)
- [ ] Maintain evidence of compliance activities
